# Bookmark rules

Written by `/setup` from this profile's bookmarks and history. Claude follows the text
below when it writes a plan; the block above is enforced by `verify-plan.py`.

## Bar shape

Eight top-level folders, at most two levels deep. The folders cover the things used
every week; everything else lives under Other bookmarks.

## Bar order

Folders left to right by how often they are used, most used first. Bookmarks inside a
folder are alphabetical.

## Protected folders

`MOBILE/Shared with Alex` is shared with someone else. Leave it exactly as it is.

## Untitled bar bookmarks

Bookmarks sitting directly on the bar without a title are icon-only buttons on purpose.
Keep them untitled and keep them on the bar.

## Duplicates

Merge exact URL duplicates only. Near-duplicates, such as the same site with a different
path, stay.

## Cold material

Bookmarks not visited in the history window go to `OTHER/Archive`, keeping their folder
names. Nothing is deleted for being unused.
